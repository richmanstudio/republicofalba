The PDF contains **five simple SAMA statements** compatible with the known vocabulary, **not** a complete legal translation of the founder's approved presidential rules. Those rules are preserved in the editorial source and decision register pending normative legal terminology in SAMA. Neither this draft nor its illustrative cover is an enacted constitution.
