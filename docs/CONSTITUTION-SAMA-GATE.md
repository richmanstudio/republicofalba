# Constitution / SAMA legal-language readiness gate

**Date:** 2026-11-22  
**Status:** IN PROGRESS — **Constitution Chapter I has not been started as a normative SAMA text.**

## Founding mandate

1. All original official documents of the Republic of Alba must be written in SAMA.
2. Russian and English materials can be editorial explanations or informational translations, not authentic originals.
3. Before drafting Chapter I, complete the legally precise language necessary for **all planned chapters**, including the syntactic structures required to state rights, powers, deadlines, exclusions and effects of enactment.
4. Do not call an unapproved translation a law, an adopted constitution, or an official SAMA original.

- [Necessary legal grammar](https://github.com/richmanstudio/worldwidesamaianlanguage/blob/main/docs/grammar/constitutional-law.md): 24 semantic/syntactic constructions to resolve.
- [ADR-0010](https://github.com/richmanstudio/worldwidesamaianlanguage/blob/main/docs/decisions/0010-alba-legal-terminology.md): methodology and limitations.

Of the 338 inventory positions, 13 currently match an existing SAMA primary Russian gloss (semantic legal review still required), 65 are linked to draft new forms and 260 remain unmapped. Some overlap across chapters is intentional. The inventory cannot be declared final until full article wording is approved.

## Entry conditions for Chapter I drafting

- [ ] All concepts needed for all chapter drafts have an accepted SAMA form/sense with defined legal scope.
- [ ] All constitutional use cases can be expressed in accepted grammar, including negation scope, exceptions, entitlement, number of consecutive presidential terms, commencement and repeal.
- [ ] No unresolved false friends, collisions, or ambiguous legal role assignments in the targeted formulations.
- [ ] All legal examples are checked by grammar tests and semantic contrasts.
- [ ] Independent editorial legal review and bilingual back-translation have been performed, or their absence and consequences made explicit.
- [ ] The canonical SAMA release, dictionary and lessons are synchronized.

**Do not automatically check these boxes when a candidate file is added.**
